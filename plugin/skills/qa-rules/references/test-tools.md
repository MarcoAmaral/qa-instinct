# Test Tools Rules & Guidelines

> **ISTQB CTFL v4.0 Chapter 6** | Tool Categories | Benefits & Risks of Automation | Choosing and Justifying a Tool

This ruleset governs how the AI helps an analyst choose, justify or introduce a test tool, and how it describes what a tool will and will not do. Apply it before recommending a tool and before proposing to automate a body of manual tests.

---

- Name the activity a tool makes cheaper, and its category (CTFL §6.1), before naming a product.
- Never present a tool as a guarantee of quality: testing shows the presence of defects, not their absence (CTFL §1.3), automated or not.

Categories, benefits, risks and how to recommend a tool: the `qa-reference` skill's `references/test-tools.md`.
